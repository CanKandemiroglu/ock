This repo is built around **your** background (organic geochemist, mass-spec & stable-isotope expertise, Python/R, trilingual DE/EN/TR, teaching). It gives you two complementary ways to make money, matched to your goals (15+ hrs/week, ~$0 budget, fastest first dollar):

1. **`freelance-kit/`** — the **fastest first dollar**. Sell your PhD-level skills (editing, data analysis, tutoring) on platforms that pay $30–150/hr. Cash in days, $0 to start.
2. **`index.html` + `products/`** — the **scalable asset**. A ready-to-sell storefront of **AI prompt packs for researchers** — an audience you uniquely own. Compounds in the background while the freelance work pays now.

Open **`freelance-kit/README.md`** for the full week-one playbook. It contains, all drafted from your CV:

## 🔬 Path 2 — ResearchForge (the scalable product)

A complete, deploy-ready storefront selling **AI prompt packs for grad students, PhDs and researchers** — one of the top-selling digital-product categories of 2026, and a niche where your scientist credibility *is* the marketing.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Full storefront: landing page, free **Research Prompt Generator** tool, product cards, email capture, FAQ. Zero build step. |
| `products/starter-pack-free.md` | 🎁 Free lead magnet — 10 research prompts (given for email signups) |
| `products/publish-faster-50-prompts.md` | 📄 The $19 product — 50 prompts for every stage of publishing a paper |
| `products/academic-os.md` | 🎓 The $39 flagship — 105 prompts + 5 workflows (thesis, defense, career; bundles Publish Faster) |

The products are **real, finished, and integrity-first** (every pack warns against fabricated data/citations and tells the user to verify and disclose AI use per journal policy).

1. **Host it:** GitHub Pages (repo → Settings → Pages → your branch → `/root`). Live at `https://<username>.github.io/ock/`. (Or drag into Netlify.)
2. **Payments:** free [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com) account → create two products, upload the `.md` files (export to PDF first: paste into Google Docs → Download as PDF). Sell **Publish Faster** ($19) and **Academic OS** ($39, bundle both PDFs). Replace the two `<!-- LAUNCH: replace href -->` links in `index.html` with your Gumroad URLs.
3. **Email capture:** free [Formspree](https://formspree.io) form → replace `YOUR_FORM_ID` in `index.html`. Deliver `starter-pack-free.md` (as PDF) to signups.

### Where your audience is (free traffic)
Your credibility is the edge — market where researchers gather:
1. **Reddit:** r/GradSchool, r/PhD, r/AskAcademia, r/labrats — answer real questions, share a free prompt, link the free pack in your profile (never spam the paid one).
2. **Academic X/Bluesky/LinkedIn:** post one useful prompt/day ("steal this prompt for your lit review"). Your MARUM/scientist identity builds trust fast.
3. **Niche down for more sales:** clone a pack for a specific audience you know — "AI prompts for Earth Science PhDs", "for non-native English researchers", "for thesis writing". Niche packs outsell generic ones.

## 💡 How the two paths feed each other
Freelance clients → testimonials + real pain points → better products. Product audience → freelance leads (some readers want you to *do* the work, not DIY it). Run Path 1 for income now; let Path 2 compound.

## ⚖️ A note on research integrity
The prompt products are deliberately built to *assist*, not to cheat: they make you supply the science and sources, warn about AI hallucination, and remind users to verify and disclose. That's not just ethics — it's what keeps the brand credible with an academic audience.

## 🛠 Customizing
- Site styling: CSS variables at the top of `index.html` (`--accent`, `--bg`…). Rebrand in minutes.
- Generator prompts: the `TEMPLATES` object in `index.html` — add research tasks to target more keywords.
- Rename "ResearchForge" if it's taken in your market.
