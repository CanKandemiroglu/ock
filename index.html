FAQ

Questions, answered

Isn't using AI in research cheating / against the rules?

Using AI to draft, structure and edit your own work — then verifying and citing properly — is increasingly accepted, and many journals allow it with disclosure. These prompts are built for that honest use: you provide the science and the sources, AI helps you write it faster, and you stay the author. Always check your institution's and target journal's specific AI policy. Never use AI to fabricate data, references, or results.

Will the AI make up fake references?

It can — which is exactly why these prompts are written to make you supply the sources and to flag anything unverified. Every pack includes a "no hallucinated citations" instruction and a verification checklist. Treat AI output as a smart first draft, never as fact.

Do these work for my field?

Yes. The prompts are discipline-agnostic — you fill in your field, methods and findings. They've been designed and tested against real STEM writing (geoscience, biology, chemistry) but work for social sciences and humanities too.

Which AI tool do I need?

Any of ChatGPT (free or paid), Claude, or Gemini. Every prompt is model-agnostic. For long documents, Claude and ChatGPT paid tiers handle more context.

Do I get updates?

Yes — lifetime. As models improve and prompts get sharper, you get the new versions free. Keep your receipt email.
